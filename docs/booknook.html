Book Nook

This page contains an incomplete list of books I have read or listened to, inspired by the edX course Behavioural Economics in Action that I took in Fall 2020. Most of these books have something to do with economics (which was not entirely by choice) and/or data analytics. This list is ongoing and is being updated periodically.
